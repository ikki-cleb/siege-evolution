# Age rating and privacy answers

Prepared answers for each store's questionnaire. Both stores reword these
forms from time to time, so match each answer by meaning, not exact wording.
The user chose a **13+ target audience** (like Trivia Ladder), which keeps the
game out of Google's "Designed for Families" program.

## What's in the game

Cartoon tower defense: units shoot, slow, stun and poison cartoon invaders
(bugs, animals, germs, toy soldiers, aliens, kitchen pests, party toys), who
pop or crumble with a puff when defeated. Bosses explode or topple. There's no
blood, no realistic violence, no human death, no text chat and no ads.

Typical result: ESRB Everyone 10+, PEGI 7.

**Target audience ("Target audience and content"):** 13–15, 16–17 and 18+.
Leaving under-13s out avoids the Families Policy requirements.
**Does the app appeal to children?** Answer honestly: the cartoon art may
appeal to kids, but the game isn't designed for them; Google may ask follow-up
questions.

## Apple App Privacy ("nutrition label")

- **Data collection:** **Data Not Collected.** Progress stays on the device.
  Purchases are processed by Apple; the app only checks which items it owns.
  No analytics, ads, crash reporting, accounts, contacts or location.

## Google Play Data safety

- **Does the app collect or share user data?** **No.** (Purchases are handled
  by Google Play Billing; the app doesn't send any data anywhere.)
- **Ads:** No ads
- **Encrypted in transit:** not applicable (no data sent)
- **Account deletion:** not applicable (no accounts)

## Export compliance (Apple)

The game uses no encryption beyond what iOS provides. The iOS export preset
already adds `ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption = NO` to the app's Info.plist
(`application/additional_plist_content`), so App Store Connect won't ask about
encryption on each upload.
