Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This policy explains what information the Siege Evolution app ("the app"), published by [PUBLISHER] ("we"), handles. Short version: we don't collect personal information, show ads or track you.

Information stored on your device

Your progress, unlocked worlds, achievements, endless-mode records and settings are saved only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app deletes them.

Purchases

Optional extras are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play, and those companies handle your payment details under their own privacy policies. The app only learns which items you own, so it can unlock them and restore them on a new device. We never see your name, email or payment information.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, sign-in or contact details
  • No advertising or ad identifiers
  • No analytics, crash reporting or tracking across other apps or websites
  • No selling or sharing of personal information

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
