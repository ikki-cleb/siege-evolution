From the backyard to the stars.

Siege Evolution is a tower defense game for iPhone and Android. Build a maze of units, evolve them down two branching paths, collect relics, and hold the line against bugs, beasts, germs, toy armies and an alien armada. Then see how far you get in endless mode.

7 worlds, 35 levels. Backyard Bugs, Wild Kingdom, Microscopic, Total War and Galactic Siege, plus two bonus worlds.

Evolve everything. Every unit branches into a power or utility evolution, then a capstone.

Free to play, no ads. Optional extras are one-time purchases. No account, no tracking.
