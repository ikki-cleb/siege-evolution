Restoring purchases

Bought something on another phone, or reinstalled the game? Open Store → Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

My progress is gone

Progress is saved only on your device, so deleting the app or switching phones starts a fresh game. Purchases always come back with Restore Purchases.

Refunds

Purchases are handled by Apple and Google. Request a refund through Apple or Google Play.

Tips

  • Units block invaders: build a maze to make their route longer.
  • Tap a unit that survived a wave to evolve it. Tap an evolution twice: the first tap previews it.
  • Defeat the glowing invader to earn a relic.
  • Beat a world's boss to unlock endless mode there.
